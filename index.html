Hardware Details

The red, yellow, and blue connectors you’re thinking of are insulated crimp connectors / terminals (commonly called spade connectors, ring terminals, butt connectors, etc., depending on the style).

The color coding doesn’t refer to voltage or function, but rather the wire size (AWG / mm²) they’re designed for:

🔵🟡🔴 Color Code (Standard)

  • Red → for 22–16 AWG wire (0.5–1.5 mm²)
  • Blue → for 16–14 AWG wire (1.5–2.5 mm²)
  • Yellow → for 12–10 AWG wire (4–6 mm²)

These colors are standard across most brands.

🧰 Connector Types (insulated)

  • Ring Terminals → bolt or screw passes through
  • Spade (Fork) Terminals → easy connect/disconnect under a screw
  • Butt Connectors → join two wires inline
  • Quick Disconnects (Male/Female Spades) → flat blade push-on connectors
  • Pin Terminals → small round pins for terminal blocks
  • Bullet Connectors → round push-fit male/female

🧩 Size / Fit Options

  • Stud/bolt size for rings & spades: M3, M4, M5, M6, M8, ¼″, etc.
  • Tab size for quick disconnects: Common are 6.3 mm (¼″), 4.8 mm, 2.8 mm widths.
  • Butt connectors: Sized to match wire gauge.
